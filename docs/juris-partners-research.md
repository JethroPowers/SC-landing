## Task evidence versus commercial hypotheses

The official sources establish that structured information, authorised handoffs, evidence work and review points exist. They do not establish poor performance, willingness to pay, buying urgency or a need for Juris in a specific firm. Existing practice tools can already handle many tasks.

The two Hungarian agent conversations in the brief are founder-reported evidence of those conversations only. Their focus on traffic is not a universal demand signal and does not establish that those agents manage applications. An existing-enquiry brief or follow-up queue is a candidate output, not a promised increase in traffic or conversion.

A lead-seeking visitor can discuss collaboration independently. Any operational proposal should be worth doing for its own defined output, with no promise to compensate for missing traffic.
